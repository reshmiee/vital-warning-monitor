Catch patient deterioration before it happens, not after

Aarogya forecasts the next set of patient vitals, scores them against clinical NEWS2 rules, and flags patients who are trending toward deterioration — giving clinical teams earlier time to act

How it works

A simple idea. A big difference.

1

Vitals stream in

We use a continuous stream of patient vitals, turned into minute-by-minute snapshots

2

Forecast the next reading

We predict the next set of vitals using time-series and machine learning models

3

Score it and flag early

We score the forecasted vitals against NEWS2 clinical rules and flag patients before thresholds are crossed

WHY IT MATTERS

A single snapshot shows severity, not direction.

Two patients can have the same current NEWS2 score while one is improving and the other is getting worse. By looking at the trajectory — not just the snapshot — Aarogya helps clinical teams spot deterioration earlier, so they can act sooner
